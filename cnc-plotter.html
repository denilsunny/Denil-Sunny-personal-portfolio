Project Overview

Designed and developed a 3-axis CNC drawing machine capable of converting digital designs into physical drawings. The final prototype combines an Arduino-based CNC controller, stepper-motor-driven motion, belt transmission, linear motion components, and a servo-controlled drawing mechanism.

The machine was built using a combination of custom 3D-printed components and standard mechanical hardware.

Mechanical Design

The final prototype uses a combination of 3D-printed structural and mounting components, linear bearings, steel guide rods, and a linear rail to create the axis movement system.

The stepper motors drive the GT2 timing-belt mechanism, while the bearings and guide rods constrain the movement to provide controlled linear motion. The drawing tool is mounted to the moving mechanism, with the micro servo controlling its vertical pen position.

04 /

Working Principle

The drawing process begins with a digital design that is converted into G-code, containing the required movement and drawing commands.

The Arduino Uno, running GRBL, receives and interprets the G-code. The CNC shield interfaces the controller with the TMC2208 stepper drivers, which drive the NEMA 17 motors. The motors rotate the GT2 belt-and-pulley system to move the drawing mechanism along the required axes.

At the same time, the micro servo controls the pen-up and pen-down operation, allowing the machine to draw only when required.

Limit Positioning & Power System

Limit / Reference Positioning
Two contact switches are incorporated into the machine as limit switches, providing positional reference for the moving axes. This allows the CNC system to detect the end of the permitted travel range and establish a repeatable machine position.
2× Contact Switches
Power System
The system operates from a 12 V, 2 A or higher power supply. The 12 V supply powers the CNC motion system, while the required lower-voltage electronics and accessories are supplied through the appropriate circuitry/interface within the system.
12 V · 2 A+
